Sarah Wooders

Sarah Wooders is the co-founder and CTO of Letta, building agents that learn over time, through memory that is model-agnostic and interpretable.

She holds a PhD in Computer Science from UC Berkeley, where she was advised by Ion Stoica and Joseph E. Gonzalez and focused on systems for AI. Her undergraduate degree is from MIT in computer science and mathematics.
